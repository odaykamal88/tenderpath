Expression of Interest — Emergency Logistics & Last-Mile Distribution Services

Ref: WFP/KEN/2026/EOI-0000 · Submitted via UNGM · Redacted Company Ltd

Redacted Company Ltd is a Kenyan logistics firm operating an owned fleet of 14 vehicles (3–28 t) with live tracking compliant with the agency's commodity-movement requirements, and 8,400 m² of bonded warehousing in Nairobi and Garissa. Since 2023 we have completed last-mile distribution across the ASAL counties of Garissa, Wajir, Mandera, Turkana and Marsabit — the precise operating geography of this programme — maintaining a documented 96% on-time delivery rate through the 2025 long rains.

Of particular relevance to Lot 2, our teams hold cold-chain handling experience gained on health-sector solar installation contracts with a UN agency (2024, USD 000,000) and an international NGO (2023), both of which are cited with reference contacts in Annex B. We confirm our eligibility declarations in Annex C, including sanctions-list and conflict-of-interest attestations, signed by an authorized signatory.

What you gave us: two sentences of positioning angle and a counties list. What we did: expanded it into an evidence-led narrative, mirrored the tender's own geography and lot language, and pulled two references from your vault — because agencies shortlist on evidence, not adjectives.

Compliance phrasing: the declarations paragraph tracks the agency's exact annex names, so the screener finds everything where the checklist says it should be.

What kills EOIs — and what we gate before you pay
  • Late submission · missing mandatory declarations · eligibility documents absent · wrong submission channel (this one goes through UNGM — we check your registration first).

Technical Conformity Response — Off-Grid Solar Systems, 12 Facilities

Ref: UNICEF/ITB-0000 · Technical envelope — contains no pricing

2.7O&M plan + spare parts for 24 monthsComplyO&M plan Annex T-7 · spares schedule included

Site-visit certificate: attached (mandatory) · Bid validity: 120 days as required · Bid security: bid-securing declaration, signed · Financial bid sealed separately — see Envelope B.

The deviation phrasing is the product. An undeclared deviation voids the bid at inspection; a badly-declared one fails conformity. Rows 2.2 and 2.6 are written so the deviation is honest, substantiated, and survives evaluation — that sentence structure is what 34-point compliance gating buys you.

Envelope discipline: the technical response above contains zero pricing. Financial data leaking into the technical envelope is the classic self-inflicted ITB death — our pack keeps the two structurally separate.

What kills ITBs — and what we gate before you pay
  • Unsigned bid form or missing bid security · financial info in the technical envelope · short validity · undeclared spec deviations · missed mandatory site visit.

Technical Proposal — §3 Methodology & Approach [criterion 1 · 30 pts]

Ref: UNDP/RFP-0000 · Climate Resilience Advisory · Technical envelope

Our approach is community-led adaptation delivered in co-execution with county governments, structured against the five TOR tasks. Task 1 (vulnerability mapping) applies the participatory CBA framework our team deployed on a comparable UNDP Kenya engagement (2024), producing ward-level indices the TOR's M&E annex can consume directly. Task 2 embeds county officers in each field team — addressing the TOR's sustainability criterion explicitly rather than by implication…

Workplan note [criterion 4 · 10 pts] — mobilization within 3 weeks of signature; field phases scheduled around the Oct–Nov rains, with the critical path and dependencies in Annex W.

Written against the matrix, point by point. Every section opens by answering the criterion it is scored under — evaluators award points with the matrix beside them, so the proposal is organized the way the points are. Your one-line angle becomes 8–10 pages mapped to the TOR tasks.

Team & CVs is worth 25 points. If a key-expert CV is missing at intake we flag it before you pay — upload one, have us build it from the expert's background, or we nominate from our vetted associate pool.

What kills RFPs — and what we gate before you pay
  • Financial data inside the technical envelope · scoring below the 70% technical threshold · key-expert CVs missing or unsigned · page and format limits breached.
