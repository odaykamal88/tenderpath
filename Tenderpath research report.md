Here's the honest competitive map and where TenderPath's current offering falls short:

**What the incumbents already do well:**

Devex tracks 850+ funding sources including tenders, grants, and open opportunities, offers early pipeline intelligence, forecast analysis, a database of 50,000+ organizations, and contract award/shortlist data. They also have Call4Partners for consortium building, a CV database for expert recruitment, and 20 years of institutional credibility. DevelopmentAid hosts 87,000+ NGOs, offers tender and grant discovery, forecast tenders, CV databases, and BidNexus for proposal management. UNGM Pro itself offers a Tender Alert Service with UNSPSC-based filtering and Opportunity Search Plus.

**Where TenderPath currently sits — honestly:**

Your three tiers ($49/mo tender intelligence, $299/submission package, $999/mo strategic partnership) are competing directly with Devex and DevelopmentAid on discovery, which is their strongest turf with decades of data and hundreds of sources. You won't out-aggregate them. The $49/mo tender monitoring is a race to the bottom against platforms with 20 years of sourcing infrastructure.

**The wedge you should chase instead:**

The entire market — Devex, DevelopmentAid, UNGM, Brainial, BidNexus — stops at **information and tools**. They show you opportunities and give you project management software. None of them will actually write your proposal, score your readiness against a specific tender, or tell you your realistic probability of winning. Professionals take up to six days to identify five good grant fits, and writing one application takes between 10 and 30 hours. That 30-hour pain is completely unaddressed by any platform.

**The repositioned TenderPath — "AI does the work, not just the search":**

Stop competing on discovery. Compete on **output**. Here's the restructured offering:

**Free tier (keep as-is, this is your wedge):** The website-crawl readiness assessment. No other platform does this. Devex makes you search through filters manually. You analyze the company automatically and tell them where they stand. This is genuinely differentiated.

**Tier 1 — Bid/No-Bid Analyst ($29-49 per tender):** User pastes a specific tender URL or uploads the TOR. AI reads the full document, cross-references against their company profile, and produces a detailed bid/no-bid recommendation with: win probability estimate, scoring criteria breakdown, gap analysis specific to that tender, required documents checklist, and estimated effort to submit. This doesn't exist anywhere. Devex shows you the tender — TenderPath tells you whether to bother.

**Tier 2 — Proposal Draft ($199-399 per submission):** AI generates an 80%-complete technical proposal, financial template, and compliance package tailored to the specific tender. The user reviews, adds their specifics, and submits. This collapses the 30-hour process into 3-4 hours of review and customization. Price it per submission, not per month — removes the subscription commitment barrier for SMEs.

**Tier 3 — Grants \+ Tenders Matching ($49/mo):** Keep the monitoring subscription but reposition it around the AI-matched digest, not raw alerts. Weekly email with 5-10 opportunities scored by fit, not a firehose of 200 tenders. Include grant opportunities here — this is where you expand beyond what UNGM Pro offers. Arabic \+ English.

**Tier 4 — Roster Fast-Track ($499-999 per roster):** AI prepares the complete roster/vendor registration package for a specific agency (WFP, UNICEF, UNDP, etc.). This is productized consulting at 10% of the consultant price.

**Why this wins against incumbents:**

Devex and DevelopmentAid are **information platforms**. They sell access to data. TenderPath becomes a **work-completion platform**. It does the job, not just shows the job listing. That's a category difference, not a feature difference. They're job boards — you're a staffing agency that actually fills the role.

The free assessment is the top of funnel that neither Devex nor DevelopmentAid has. Nobody else lets a company drop in a URL and get an instant readiness score. That's your viral loop — it's shareable, it's instant, and it leads directly to "okay, now help me actually apply."

Drop the $999/mo strategic partnership tier entirely for now. It requires dedicated advisor time which doesn't scale solo. Replace it with per-transaction pricing across everything. Every interaction is a discrete paid event, not a relationship you need to maintain.

**LTAS& Becoming part of the shortlist**

Both are huge. The LTA angle especially — that's the real game within the game.

**LTAs and shortlist positioning is the highest-value service you can offer.** Here's why it changes everything:

An LTA (Long-Term Agreement) is essentially a pre-approved vendor status with a specific UN agency for a specific category. Once you're on an LTA, you skip the open tender process entirely — the agency calls you directly when they need something in your category. You're on the shortlist by definition. These typically run 2-5 years and represent recurring revenue for the vendor without competing each time.

The problem is that LTA opportunities are announced irregularly, the application process is agency-specific, and most SMEs don't even know they exist as a separate pathway. Nobody is tracking LTA windows across agencies systematically.

**What to build:**

**Tier 5 — LTA & Shortlist Accelerator ($799-1,499 per application)**

This is the premium product. The AI:

* Monitors all agency LTA/framework agreement announcements across WFP, UNICEF, UNDP, UNHCR, UNOPS  
* Alerts the vendor when an LTA opportunity matches their profile  
* Prepares the complete LTA application: capability statement formatted per agency, past performance evidence mapped to their evaluation criteria, financial capacity documentation, team CVs structured to agency preferences  
* Includes a "shortlist strategy brief" — based on historical award data, which agencies have upcoming LTA renewals in their category, optimal timing to apply, and which rosters feed into which shortlists

The pitch writes itself: "One LTA application that costs you $999 can generate $500K+ in direct-invite contracts over 3-5 years. No open competition. No proposal writing each time. They call you."

Position this as the graduation path. A vendor starts with the free assessment, buys a couple of proposal packages, wins a contract or two, and then the platform recommends: "You now qualify for WFP's logistics LTA renewal opening in Q3 — want us to prepare your application?" That's a natural upsell from proven value.

**The dashboard — this is the retention moat:**

Build a vendor command center that tracks their entire procurement lifecycle. This is what turns TenderPath from a transaction platform into an operating system they can't leave.

**Dashboard sections:**

**Pipeline View** — All opportunities in one Kanban-style board: Matched → Evaluating → Preparing → Submitted → Under Review → Won/Lost. Drag opportunities through stages. Each card shows deadline, agency, estimated value, and fit score.

**Application Archive** — Every submission ever made through the platform: tender reference, agency, date submitted, proposal documents, status, outcome. This is their institutional memory. After 12 months, this archive is irreplaceable — it's their entire procurement history in one place.

**Win/Loss Analytics** — Win rate by agency, category, and contract size. Average scores from debriefs (when available). Trends over time. "Your WFP proposals score 15% higher than your UNDP proposals — here's why."

**Document Vault** — All their compliance documents, certifications, financial statements, team CVs in one place, with expiry tracking. "Your ISO 9001 expires in 45 days — this will disqualify you from 3 active opportunities." This alone justifies a subscription.

**LTA & Roster Tracker** — Visual map of which rosters and LTAs they're on, which ones are pending, which have upcoming renewal windows. Status per agency: Not Registered → Applied → Under Review → Pre-qualified → Active LTA.

**Readiness Score Over Time** — Their original assessment score tracked quarterly as they close gaps. Gamification element: "You've moved from 62 to 78 since January. You now qualify for 40% more opportunities."

**Revenue Impact** — Total contract value won through the platform. Pipeline value of active submissions. This is the number that justifies every dollar they spend with you, and it's the number you use in your own marketing.

**Why the dashboard changes the business model:**

Right now TenderPath is transactional — people pay per submission and leave. The dashboard creates a reason to stay. Once their pipeline, documents, history, and LTA tracking live in your system, switching means losing all of it. That's when the $49/mo subscription tier actually makes sense — not for tender alerts (Devex does that) but for access to *their own data and workflow*.

The revised tier structure becomes:

* **Free:** Readiness assessment (top of funnel, viral)  
* **$29-49/tender:** Bid/no-bid analysis (low commitment entry)  
* **$199-399/submission:** AI proposal package (core revenue)  
* **$49/mo:** Dashboard \+ matched digest \+ document vault (retention)  
* **$799-1,499/application:** LTA & roster accelerator (premium upsell)

The dashboard subscription is the glue. Everything else drives transactions. The dashboard drives monthly recurring revenue and creates the switching cost that makes TenderPath defensible against anyone — Devex included.
